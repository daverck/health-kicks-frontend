1 Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair my connected footwear with the mobile app?

Ensure Bluetooth and Location services are enabled on your smartphone. Power on your shoe clip by holding the side button for 2 seconds (LED blinks cyan). In the mobile app, tap Add Device and confirm the 6-digit BLE secure PIN match.

What should I do if a fall alert is accidentally triggered?

When an impact is detected, the shoe vibrates and the mobile app sounds a 30-second warning countdown. You can tap Cancel Alert / I am fine on either the mobile screen or double-click the shoe button to abort escalation to your emergency contacts.

How long does the battery last on the ESP32-S3 clip?

Thanks to dynamic FreeRTOS light sleep between 19 Hz IMU sampling bursts, the 450 mAh LiPo battery provides up to 36 hours of continuous active biomechanical tracking.
