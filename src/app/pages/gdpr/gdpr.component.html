European Union General Data Protection Regulation (EU 2016/679) Compliance Matrix

1 GDPR Architecture & Rights Matrix

HealthKicks is engineered under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation EU 2016/679), incorporating Privacy by Design and by Default (Article 25). Below is how our platform implements your statutory data subject rights (Articles 15 through 22) as well as technical security and governance mandates (Articles 30 and 32):

Right to Access & Data Portability (Articles 15 & 20)

Export your full profile, paired devices, step history, and fall alerts as a standardized ZIP archive with an authenticated request.

Profile Export

Right to Erasure / Right to be Forgotten (Article 17)

Permanently purge your account, paired device tokens, step metrics, and emergency contacts. An immutable audit record tracks execution without storing PII.

Delete Account

Security of Processing & Multi-Tier Cryptography (Article 32)

In strict accordance with GDPR Article 32(1)(a), HealthKicks implements layered technical safeguards spanning embedded sensors, mobile edge gateway, and cloud persistence:

BLE GATT Security

LESC (Low Energy Secure Connections) with numeric comparison pairing prevents MITM eavesdropping on 19 Hz IMU packets.

Mobile & In-Transit

Hardware-backed encrypted SQLite on mobile (KeyStore/Keychain) and TLS 1.3 / AWS SigV4 signed WebSockets for MQTT telemetry streams.

Cloud at Rest & Audit

AES-256 encryption at rest (Aurora PostgreSQL & DynamoDB EU) combined with append-only immutable database audit triggers.
