HealthKicks Privacy & Data Protection Commitment — Last revised: November 2026

1 Data Controller & Principles

HealthKicks is committed to protecting your personal data, biometric observations, and physical telemetry. We act as Data Controller under EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR). Our core guiding principle is data minimization: sensor raw data is processed at the edge whenever possible, and only required metrics are transmitted to cloud storage.

2 Categories of Data We Process

Account & Profile Data

Name, email address, authenticated identity provider (Google/Microsoft), role (User, Trainer, Administrator), and user preferences.

Sensor Telemetry, Location & Falls

Step cadence, daily counts, physical activity classifications, fall alert timestamps, emergency acknowledgements, device battery levels, and optional GPS coordinates (captured only when an emergency fall alert is triggered and location permission is granted).

Health Connect (Android)

With your explicit permission, the mobile app uses Google Health Connect (READ_STEPS and WRITE_STEPS) solely to synchronize your daily step counts. Health Connect data is never sold or shared for advertising.

Application Diagnostics (Sentry)

Error stack traces and performance diagnostics collected via Sentry with automatic PII, email, and token redaction to ensure service stability.

Web Analytics (Umami)

Aggregated, anonymous usage metrics (page visits, referrer source, browser/OS family) collected via Umami Cloud without cookies, persistent identifiers, or personal data tracking.

3 Hosting & Infrastructure Security

All relational and time-series data is stored within encrypted data centers in the European Union (AWS Europe (Ireland, eu-west-1): Amazon Aurora PostgreSQL and AWS DynamoDB). All transmissions over the wire use TLS 1.3 / SigV4 encrypted WebSockets. BLE communications between the footwear clip and smartphone use LESC (Low Energy Secure Connections) with numeric comparison pairing.

4 Wellness & Non-Medical Disclaimer

HealthKicks is a physical activity and wellness monitoring companion. It is not a certified medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition. Emergency fall alerts are best-effort notifications and do not replace emergency services.

5 Exercising Your Privacy Rights & Account Deletion

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, export, and permanently delete your personal information directly within the application:

How to delete your HealthKicks account:

  1. Open the HealthKicks mobile app or web dashboard (Profile).
  2. Navigate to Danger Zone → Delete Account and confirm permanent deletion.
  3. Your account profile, device bindings, emergency contacts, and fall alerts are permanently erased. Historical ML studio sessions are irreversibly anonymized.
  4. If you cannot sign in, email privacy@healthkicks.eu from your registered address to request deletion.
